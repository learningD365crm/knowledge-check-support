# Knowledge Check — App Support

Welcome to the official support page for **Knowledge Check**, developed by **Knowledge Realm**.

## About the App
**Knowledge Check** is an interactive quiz and learning app that lets you test your knowledge across multiple categories, track your points and accuracy history, and compete on the leaderboard.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**How do I get started?**
You can jump straight into questions as a **Guest**, or tap **Log in** on the home screen to sync your points history and leaderboard progress across sessions.

**How does scoring work?**
* **Correct answer:** +1 point
* **Incorrect answer:** -1 point

**My points or progress didn't update immediately. What should I do?**
Tap the **Refresh** button in the *My Points Summary* card on the home screen to fetch your latest stats.
